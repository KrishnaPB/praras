Mechanism of Action

Chemical Cleaners vs. Living Probiotics

Why traditional chemical disinfectants fail to provide sustained hygiene and how Airbliss probiotic biology provides continuous protection.

⚠️

Traditional Chemicals & Acids

  • Temporary Kill Window: Only kills surface bacteria for 15–30 minutes before evaporating.
  • Rapid Pathogen Rebound: Pathogenic bacteria rebound quickly on clean, unoccupied surface space.
  • Biofilm Resistance: Caustic chemicals cannot penetrate thick biofilms and leave resistant colonies behind.
  • Infrastructure Degradation: Corrodes plumbing, weakens grouting, tarnishes chrome fixtures, and produces toxic VOC fumes.
  • Septic Shock: Kills beneficial sewage bacteria in STPs, increasing sludge build-up and causing regulatory non-compliance.
🌿

Airbliss Probiotic Bio-Shield

  • Continuous 24/7 Action: Beneficial spore strains remain alive and actively cleaning for up to 72 hours.
  • Competitive Exclusion: Probiotics colonize the micro-pores, starving harmful bacteria of nutrients and space.
  • Microscopic Digestion: Extracellular enzymes digest biofilm matrices, uric acid encrustations, and grease traps.
  • 100% Non-Corrosive: Completely safe on tiles, marble, epoxy, copper, PVC, and sensitive electronic environments.
  • STP & ETP Friendly: Effluent run-off actively enriches wastewater treatment plants, reducing BOD, COD, and sludge.
