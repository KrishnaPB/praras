Distillery & Fermentation Aids

Advanced Biochemical Solutions for Commercial Distilleries

Praras® is a leading manufacturer of specialty distillery additives and enzymes, offering a comprehensive portfolio designed to optimize starch conversion kinetics, accelerate fermentation cycles, and eliminate processing bottlenecks.

Our FERMAID® line provides substantial improvement in yeast vitality, minimizes diacetyls and volatile acidity, and protects against stress during condensate water reuse. The SOLUBAID® and SACCARAID® ranges deliver rapid high-temperature liquefaction and complete saccharification, maximizing alcohol yield from varied grain and molasses feedstocks.

PROVEN DISTILLERY VALUE

Why Commercial Distilleries Partner with Praras

Tailored biochemical solutions engineered to maximize alcohol yield, accelerate throughput, and ensure unyielding spirit quality.

01

Higher Alcohol Yield

Maximize fermentable sugar uptake from high-gravity molasses and diverse grain grists, minimizing unfermented residual sugar in spent wash.

02

104–107°C Jet Cook Stability

Specialized thermostable endo-amylases withstand severe thermal and pressure shear in jet cookers, achieving rapid mash thinning in under 5 minutes.

03

+10% Working Headspace

Food-grade defoamers and antifoam emulsions suppress vigorous fermenter foam in <10 seconds, eliminating wash overflow and enabling process intensification.

04

Condensate Water Reuse

Proprietary bio-nutrient complexes neutralize inhibitory volatile acids and osmotic stresses when reusing recycled condensate water in zero liquid discharge plants.

05

Dextran & Viscosity Breakdown

High-potency fungal dextranases cleave recalcitrant dextran polymers in degraded juice/molasses, preventing heat exchanger fouling and distillation scaling.

06

FSSAI & Halal Certified

All distillery enzymes and biochemical processing aids are manufactured in FSSC 22000 certified facilities conforming to FSSR 2011 & international standards.
