Why Commercial Distilleries Partner with Praras

Tailored biochemical solutions engineered to maximize alcohol yield, accelerate throughput, and ensure unyielding spirit quality.

01

Higher Alcohol Yield

Maximize fermentable sugar uptake from high-gravity molasses and diverse grain grists, minimizing unfermented residual sugar in spent wash.

02

104–107°C Jet Cook Stability

Specialized thermostable endo-amylases withstand severe thermal and pressure shear in jet cookers, achieving rapid mash thinning in under 5 minutes.

03

+10% Working Headspace

Food-grade defoamers and antifoam emulsions suppress vigorous fermenter foam in <10 seconds, eliminating wash overflow and enabling process intensification.

02

Viscosity & Dextran Control

Hydrolyzes complex dextrans to eliminate process viscosity, pan scaling, and distillation inefficiencies

1 Formulation
Enzymatic Viscosity Solution

Overcoming Dextran Inefficiencies in Cane Molasses

Degraded cane juice and high-Brix molasses contain elevated dextran concentrations that elevate process viscosity, foul heat exchangers, and hinder yeast attenuation. Enzymatic cleavage breaks down dextrans into smaller oligosaccharides for fluid process kinetics.

Hydrolyzes α-1,6 glucosidic linkages in high-molecular weight dextrans
Prevents evaporator fouling, column scaling & wash foaming
Improves molasses exhaustion and boosts final spirit recovery
