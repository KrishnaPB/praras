EGG FREE NOUGAT & FRAPPE

HiFoamAid®: Foaming Aid for Frappe Making

The HiFoamAid® range of products is a combination of specialized egg-free emulsifying agents that impart excellent smoothness and decreased density to frappe, significantly enhancing nougat and marshmallow quality.

Available in Vegan (Plant-Based), Dairy, and Non-Dairy formulations, HiFoamAid® breaks down fat globules for uniform texture, reduces frappe density for higher volume, and maintains frappe height for up to 60 minutes without foam collapse.
