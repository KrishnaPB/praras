01
The Logo

One mark, used with care

The logo is fixed — never redrawn, recoloured, stretched or rearranged. It performs best on light and cream surfaces, where the maroon and orange carry their full weight.

Primary — full colour on white / cream. The default for almost everything.
On colour / photography — place the logo inside a white “chip” so it stays exactly as designed.
Clear space — keep a margin equal to the height of the “P” free of other elements on all sides.

Do

  • Use on light, low-noise backgrounds
  • Scale proportionally only
  • Give it room to breathe
  • Use the white chip over busy imagery

Don't

  • Recolour or add effects/shadows
  • Stretch, rotate or condense
  • Place maroon directly on dark fields
  • Rebuild the wordmark in another font
Minimum size — never below 28 mm wide in print or 120 px on screen, to keep the molecular detail legible.
