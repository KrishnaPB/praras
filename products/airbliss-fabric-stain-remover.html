Multi-Enzymatic Hydrolysis of Stubborn Organic Stains

Harsh chlorine bleaches and caustic stain removers degrade cotton tensile strength and cause fabric yellowing. AIRBLISS™ Fabric Stain Remover uses targeted proteases and lipases to break protein and lipid stain molecules without fiber damage.

Digests stubborn blood, gravy, coffee, sweat, makeup, and collar stains at neutral pH

Effective in cold and warm water wash cycles, delivering significant laundry energy savings
100% chlorine-free, phosphate-free, and safe on dyed commercial uniforms
Standard Operating Procedure

Application Protocol & Guidelines

01
Pre-Spotting

Apply a small amount directly onto the stained fabric area and rub gently.

02
Dwell Time

Allow enzymes to penetrate the fabric weave for 5–10 minutes.

03
Machine Wash

Wash with regular laundry detergent in cold or warm cycle (30°C–50°C).
