Vertical Surface Cling with Continuous Probiotic Colonization

Standard liquid toilet cleaners run off vertical surfaces immediately. AIRBLISS™ UTC clings to porcelain bowl walls, ensuring prolonged enzymatic contact while depositing probiotic spores.

Viscous bio-gel formulation clings to vertical porcelain walls and rims

Standard Operating Procedure

Application Protocol & Guidelines

01
Application

Direct nozzle under the toilet bowl rim and squeeze evenly around the entire circumference.

02
Dwell Time

Allow the clinging bio-gel to work for 10–15 minutes to enzymatically lift stains.

03
Brush & Flush

Brush bowl surfaces lightly and flush for a sparkling, bio-protected finish.
