Biological Kinetics

How Living Biology Outperforms Chemicals

Conventional chemical treatments provide temporary masking while destroying beneficial biomass. AIRBLISS™ STP ACTIV™ deploys an engineered biological consortium that works in three distinct kinetic phases.

01

Rapid Acclimatization (0–12h)

Dormant microbial spores germinate rapidly in the presence of organic effluent, producing millions of active bio-enzymatic units that colonize aeration tank surfaces.

02

Enzymatic Lysis & Digestion (12–36h)

Extracellular lipases and proteases actively break down fats, oils, grease (FOG), and stubborn foam-producing scum layers into bio-available simple nutrients.

03

Bio-Equilibrium & Sludge Drop (36h+)

Beneficial probiotic bacteria achieve ecological dominance, stabilizing MLSS, oxidizing malodor molecules, and slashing secondary sludge volume by up to 70%.
