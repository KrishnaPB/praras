01 — The Idea

Clean, reimagined as something alive.

Conventional cleaners scorch the earth — wiping out every microbe, only for the bad ones to rebound. AIRBLISS does the opposite. Our products seed surfaces with beneficial microbes and plant-derived ingredients that crowd out the bad, breaking down grime, odours and allergens continuously. The result is healthier surfaces, fresher indoor air, and a gentler footprint — at home and at work.

Mission

To make every space healthier from the inside out — replacing harsh chemistry with living, plant-powered clean that’s safe for people and kind to the planet.

Promise

Clean that keeps working. Beneficial microbes stay active after application, so surfaces and air stay fresher for longer — not just for the minute after you spray.

Positioning

The trustworthy, science-backed face of probiotic cleaning — premium enough for facilities teams, warm and simple enough for the family kitchen.

Personality
Natural

Plant-powered and honest. Earthy, never clinical.

Fresh

Light, airy, optimistic — a clean breath of relief.

Trustworthy

Science-backed and transparent. We prove what we claim.

Caring

Safe around families, pets, food and the people who clean.

Effortless

Clean that quietly keeps working. That’s the bliss.

02 02 — Logo

One bloom, three ideas.

The mark is a tri-petal air-bloom: read it as a flower (nature), as circulating fresh air (the “air”), and as a probiotic bloom of good microbes (the science). The open ring is a breath of air orbiting the bloom. The wordmark is set in Jost with generous tracking — open, light and breathable.

Stacked lockup
Reversed
Icon / bloom

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the bloom icon. Never place type, edges or imagery inside this zone. Minimum width: 120px (digital) / 26mm (print) for the horizontal lockup; the icon alone may go as small as 20px.

App / favicon

What not to do

✕

Don’t recolour

Keep the logo in approved palette colours only.

✕

Don’t distort

Never stretch, squash or rescale axes independently.

✕

Don’t rotate

Keep the lockup level. The bloom may not spin off-axis.

✕

Don’t add effects

No drop shadows, glows, gradients or bevels.

✕

Don’t crowd it

Respect clear space; never let elements touch the mark.

✕

Don’t rebuild it

Don’t swap the font, re-space, or rearrange the lockup.

06 06 — Green Claims

Earned trust, not greenwashing.

Our whole premise is being better for people and planet — so our environmental claims must be airtight. Vague or unprovable claims erode trust and create legal risk, especially in B2B tenders. Four rules govern every claim we make.

01

Be specific, not vague

Replace “eco-friendly” with the actual fact: “readily biodegradable surfactants”, “bottle made from 100% recycled plastic”.

02

Substantiate every claim

If we can’t cite a test, standard or certification, we don’t print it. Keep evidence on file for B2B due-diligence.

03

Avoid absolutes

No “zero impact”, “completely natural”, “100% safe”. Use measured, honest language.

04

Name your proof

Reference real certifications and standards (e.g. eco-label, biodegradability standard, cruelty-free mark) — never invent badges.
