Pulp Suspension & Texture Science

Engineered Precision for Juice Manufacturers

Maintaining uniform fruit pulp distribution, stable cloudiness, and preventing unsightly serum separation are critical technical challenges in commercial fruit beverage manufacturing. Praras® offers specialized hydrocolloid systems that create an invisible stabilizing network.

Our solutions withstand high-shear mixing, thermal pasteurization (HTST/UHT), and hot-fill bottling — ensuring pristine visual appeal, smooth mouthfeel, and extended commercial shelf life across all fruit juice categories.

Application Protocols

Optimized for Every Fruit Beverage Category

Tailored stabilization and foam enhancement systems calibrated for popular fruit drink profiles

Pulpy Nectars

Mango, Guava & Lychee

High pulp-bearing tropical nectars requiring strong yield stress and pseudoplastic viscosity to suspend heavy fruit fibers evenly.

Recommended Solution:
