CAKE PREMIXES AND CONCENTRATE

Advanced Volume & Texture for Eggless Cakes

Eggless cake premix is a convenient, high-performance option for industrial bakeries looking to cater to vegetarian consumers and egg-free dietary preferences. In India and global markets, vegetarianism is rapidly gaining momentum, making eggless muffins and sponge cakes a high-growth segment.

PRECCEL® 93 (incorporating PRECCEL M for muffins and PRECCEL S for sponge cakes) offers bakers superior functionality, emulsification, and aeration stability while maintaining traditional baking workflows.

BAKING SOLUTION

PRECCEL® 93

Milk-based eggless cake premix concentrate engineered for muffin and sponge cakes. Delivers superior cake volume, crumb softness, and reproducible batch stability with zero deviation from standard baking workflows.

  • Nature: Milk Based Concentrate
  • Active Blend: Food-Grade Hydrocolloid & Emulsifier Matrix
  • Benefits: +4.9% cake weight gain, +13% batter gravity, extended crumb freshness
