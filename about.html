Our Heritage

Building India's Premier
Specialty Bio-Science Catalyst

Established on May 3, 2007 in Bengaluru, Praras® Biosciences was founded on a distinct conviction: industrial processing needed biological, green alternatives to heavy synthetic chemicals and cost-prohibitive imported additives.

Over nearly two decades of scientific dedication, PBPL has transformed from an agile enzyme research facility into a comprehensive bio-science powerhouse. We bridge the gap between deep biochemistry and large-scale industrial execution, formulating bespoke processing aids for bakers, brewmasters, distillers, confectioners, and food processors across the globe.

Scientific Engine

DSIR-Recognized Research & Development

Our In-House R&D Centre is formally recognized by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR), Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India.

Biocatalysis

Enzyme Engineering & Fermentation

Screening and optimizing fungal, bacterial, and plant-derived enzyme complexes. We tailor alpha-amylases, proteases, beta-glucanases, xylanases, and pectinases for specific thermal and pH performance curves.

  • Submerged & solid-state fermentation scaling
  • Enzyme kinetics and thermal inactivation profiling
  • Custom stabilized liquid & dry powder complexes
Food Rheology

Baking & Food Texture Application Lab

Complete pilot bakery and texture analytical suite. We simulate industrial production conditions to evaluate dough extensibility, crumb structure, moisture retention, and egg-free aeration systems.

  • Texture Profile Analysis (TPA) & firmness testing
  • Gluten strengthening and dough relaxation enzymes
  • Hydrocolloid synergy for dairy & eggless emulsions
Brewing & Distilling

Fermentation Diagnostics & Yield Acceleration

Pilot mashing and micro-distillation units dedicated to maximizing fermentable extract yields, accelerating attenuation, clarifying wort, and eliminating non-biological colloidal haze in beers and wines.

  • High-gravity mashing & adjunct conversion diagnostics
  • Silica hydrogel & PVPP-alternative colloidal stabilization
  • Enological nutrient balancing & pectinase extraction
Airbliss™ Science

Environmental Probiotics & Living Solutions

Pioneering microbial solutions for institutional facilities, wastewater remediation, and biological odor elimination. Our living Bacillus consortia out-compete pathogenic biofilms and degrade organic sludge.

  • STP/ETP wastewater BOD/COD reduction diagnostics
  • Enzymatic uric acid degradation & grease-trap clearance
