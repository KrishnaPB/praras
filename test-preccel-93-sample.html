Overview & Market Need

Empowering Commercial Bakeries with Premium Eggless Cakes

Eggless cake premix is a convenient option for those who prefer or need to avoid eggs in their baked goods. Cakes have this far been restricted to the egg-eating consumers. In India, there is a large segment of society who are non-egg eaters but would still like to savour the taste of cakes/pastries. Moreover, vegetarianism is gaining importance today and the demand for eggless items is certainly on the rise.

PrARAS® eggless product 'PRECCEL M' is an eggless premix concentrate for muffin cakes and 'PRECCEL S' is an eggless premix concentrate for sponge cake, developed based on changing tastes. PRECCEL 93 is a proprietary milk-protein and specialized cellulose blend that allows industrial bakeries to eliminate eggs completely without compromising on cake rise, sponginess, or crumb elasticity.

Specialized Range Variants

PRECCEL M

Concentrate formulated specifically for muffin cakes, delivering dome rise & moist center stability.

PRECCEL S

Concentrate tailored for sponge cakes, ensuring even cell structure, sliceability & volume expansion.

Key Functional Benefits

  • Weight Increase: Boosts overall finished cake weight by 4.9% per batch through superior moisture retention.
  • Batter Specific Gravity: Increases specific gravity by 13%, ensuring optimal batter consistency during depositors & baking lines.
  • Specific Volume Control: Reduces cake density by 16.36%, providing a lighter, airier, and softer mouthfeel.
  • Minimal Method Deviation: User-friendly formulation that plugs directly into existing bakery mixing systems.
  • Consistent Quality: Eliminates egg price volatility and seasonal quality variations.
