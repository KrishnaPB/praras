WAFERS / CONES

Advanced Enzyme Wafer Batter Conditioning

Biscamaze® offers advanced enzyme solutions, featuring high-performance alpha-amylase and bacterial protease, expertly designed for commercial wafer sheets and ice cream cones. These premium dough conditioners optimize dough machinability and processing characteristics to streamline your production.

By efficiently breaking down starches and proteins, Biscamaze® creates exceptionally tender, crisp, and flaky wafers. It significantly improves texture and browning for a visually appealing product, ensuring superior quality and reduced manufacturing costs.
