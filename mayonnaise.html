MAYONNAISE & DRESSINGS

Advanced Eggless Emulsion Stabilization

Mayonnaise is a beloved condiment, but traditional egg-based recipes pose challenges for the growing vegetarian and vegan markets. PREMAYCEL provides the perfect solution as a premium, eggless vegan-based premix designed to deliver a consistent, delicious taste profile.

Available in both convenient ready-to-use formats and highly efficient concentrates, PREMAYCEL offers mayonnaise manufacturers superior functionality and emulsion stability. This easy-to-use solution ensures highly reproducible performance while requiring minimal deviation from traditional preparation methods.
