The Future of
Eggless Baking
is Here

Eggless cake premix is a convenient option for those who prefer or need to avoid eggs in their baked goods. Cakes have this far been restricted to the egg-eating consumers. In India, there is a large segment of society who are non-egg eaters but would still like to savour the taste of cakes/pastries.

Moreover, vegetarianism is gaining importance today and the demand for eggless items is certainly on the rise. PrARAS® eggless product 'PRECCEL M' is an eggless premix concentrate for muffin cakes and 'PRECCEL S' is an eggless premix concentrate for sponge cake, developed based on changing tastes.
