Precision Oil Reduction & Crunchiness

The food industry faces the constant challenge of improving the quality of extruded and fried snacks while keeping production costs firmly under control. TextureAid® CLF provides the ideal solution as a premium, food-grade dough conditioner.

By significantly reducing fat uptake and enhancing overall product quality, TextureAid® CLF guarantees superior crispiness and reduced oiliness. This highly cost-effective additive directly improves customer perception and satisfaction for all extruded and fried snack products.
