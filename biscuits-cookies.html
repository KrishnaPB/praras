BISCUITS / COOKIES

Advanced Enzyme Dough Conditioning

Biscamaze® is an advanced enzyme-based dough conditioner featuring bacterial amylase and protease to optimize commercial biscuit and cracker production. It expertly enhances dough machinability, crispiness, and browning, ensuring superior baking quality, extended shelf life, and highly cost-effective manufacturing results.

Baking Solution

BISCAMAZE® LF

Enhances browning and crispiness, boosts stack height by 5%, cuts fat up to 20% and SMBS by 40–60%—all with cost-effective efficiency.

  • Activity: NLT 12000 TU/g

  • Nature: Bacterial Amylase & Protease
