BIOLOGICAL ENVIRONMENTAL SANITATION

The Power of Beneficial Bacteria

PrARAS® harnesses naturally occurring, non-pathogenic bacterial strains and high-potency biocatalysts to break down complex organic waste at the molecular level. Unlike traditional chemical cleaners that mask odors and corrode infrastructure, our living microbial formulations actively consume contaminants.

From commercial kitchen grease traps and high-footfall washrooms to heavy-load municipal and industrial sewage treatment plants (STPs/ETPs), our targeted bio-solutions deliver continuous 24/7 self-sustaining cleanliness with zero toxic residues.
