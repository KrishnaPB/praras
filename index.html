How Airbliss works

Probiotics clean differently. Here's why that matters.

Conventional cleaners kill everything on contact. Airbliss uses live probiotic cultures that establish themselves on surfaces, crowding out pathogens naturally.

01

Apply

Spray or mop the Airbliss solution onto the surface. Probiotic cultures are delivered in their active state, ready to work immediately.

02

Colonise

Beneficial bacteria establish a living colony on the surface, bio-competitively excluding harmful pathogens and odour-causing organisms.

03

Digest

Enzymes secreted by probiotic strains break down organic matter — the root cause of persistent odour and biofilm — rather than masking it.

04

Protect

The probiotic colony continues working for days after application, providing residual protection that no chemical cleaner can match.
