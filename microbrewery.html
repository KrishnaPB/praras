Small-Batch Optimized

Dosage rates and package sizes calibrated specifically for 5 HL to 50 HL batch sizes, with zero minimum order quantities (MOQ) for independent brewpubs.

02

Unlocks Complex Grain Bills

Powerful beta-glucanases and proteases degrade viscous beta-glucans in wheat, rye, and unmalted oats, preventing stuck mashes and shortening lautering run-off times.

03

Crystal Clarity Without Stripping Hops

Targeted clarifying hydrogels and kettle finings remove haze-active polyphenols and proteins cleanly, ensuring crystal brilliance while leaving hop terpenes untouched.

04

High-Gravity Fermentation Power

Specialized yeast foods provide vital Free Amino Nitrogen (FAN), zinc, and sterols to power high-gravity stouts, IPAs, and Belgian ales to complete, clean attenuation.

05

Active Krausen Control

Food-grade liquid antifoam prevents violent krausen overflows in unitanks and open fermenters, maximizing usable tank capacity by up to 15% without affecting head foam.

All products meet Indian food safety and excise compliance standards with Certificate of Analysis (COA) documentation and direct brewmaster technical consultation.

02

Kettle & Whirlpool Finings

High-grade kappa carrageenan for tight trub compaction and brilliant hot wort clarity

1 Formulation
Hot Side Clarification

Brilliant Wort & Compact Trub Cone

Kettle boiling releases large volumes of coagulable haze proteins. Our purified carrageenan finings bind electrostatically with positive protein charges during the boil, creating heavy flocs that settle into a dense, tight trub cone in the whirlpool.

High gel strength (NLT 300 g/cm²) for rapid flocculation
Maximizes cast-out wort volume by reducing trub sediment losses
Improves subsequent yeast health and downstream filtration

1 Formulation
Colloidal Stabilization

Selective Haze Adsorption & Foam Preservation

Craft beers served fresh from the brite tank or keg often develop unappealing chill haze when cold. Our specialized silica hydrogel selectively adsorbs only the sensitive haze-forming hordein proteins without affecting head retention proteins or hop bitterness.

Selectively adsorbs haze-active proteins with zero color stripping
Guarantees zero negative impact on beer foam stability or lacing
Completely insoluble and removed 100% during cold settling or filtration
