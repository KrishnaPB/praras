Standard Operating Procedure

Application Protocol & Guidelines

01
Shock Dose for Upset Plants

Add 30–50 ppm based on aeration tank volume daily for the first 3–5 days to establish dominant bacterial biomass.

02
Maintenance Dosing

Dose 10–20 ppm daily into the aeration basin or equalization tank inlet during peak inflow hours.

03
Aeration Monitoring

Maintain dissolved oxygen (DO) between 1.5–2.5 mg/L in aerobic basins for optimum metabolic kinetics.
