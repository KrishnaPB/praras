Why Choose Praras®

Engineering Higher Quality & Profitability

Our enological additives combine biochemical precision with cost optimization for modern commercial wineries

01

Higher Free-Run & Press Yields

Advanced pectinases degrade complex fruit cell walls rapidly, boosting free-run and total juice extraction by 10–15% while reducing press cycle times.

02

Clean Kinetic Fermentation

Balanced DAP and 100% organic autolyzed yeast nutrients provide complete Free Amino Nitrogen (FAN) to prevent stuck fermentations and hydrogen sulfide (H2S) off-flavors.

03

Deep Color & Phenolic Stability

High-purity enological tannins bind free anthocyanins during maceration, locking in deep red pigmentation and safeguarding delicate phenolic structures from oxidation.

04

Rapid Fining & Compact Lees

Hydrocolloid-based fining systems accelerate yeast and particulate settlement, producing tight, compact trub beds that significantly minimize wine loss during racking.

05

Preserves Varietal Terroir & Bouquet

Carefully selected enzyme and yeast profiles protect natural fruit monoterpenes and volatile esters, delivering vibrant aromatic typicity from grape to bottle.

06

FSSAI & OIV Compliant

Manufactured under strict ISO 9001, ISO 22000, and FSSC standards, fully compliant with international enological purity benchmarks and Certificate of Analysis (COA) traceability.
