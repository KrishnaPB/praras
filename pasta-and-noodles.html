High-Performance Extrusion Dough Conditioning

The TextureAid® series offers a versatile range of premium dough conditioners and texturizers expertly formulated for extruded foods, fried snacks, and restructured products. These high-performance additives optimize manufacturing by enhancing structural integrity and moisture retention.

Whether minimizing cracking and boosting springiness in pasta and noodles, reducing fat uptake in snacks, or improving texture stability, TextureAid® guarantees superior product quality and significant cost reductions for commercial producers.

Dough Conditioner

TEXTUREAID® 025

Reduces cracks and breakages in extruded food products, ensuring smooth extrusion and superior structural durability.

  • Where Used: Pasta & Noodles
  • Activity: Extrusion Conditioning Matrix
  • Nature: Hydrocolloid Blend
  • Benefits: Reduces cracks and breakages in extruded food products, preventing cooking breakage and surface micro-cracks.
Texturizer & Conditioner

TEXTUREAID® M

Conditions dough to prevent cracks and breakages, delivering pasta with greater hardness, springiness, and lasting quality.

  • Where Used: Pasta & Noodles
  • Activity: NLT 50000 IU/g
  • Nature: Psyllium Husk Based
  • Benefits: Conditions dough to prevent cracks and breakages, delivering pasta with greater hardness, springiness, and lasting quality.
Hydrocolloid Texturizer

TEXTUREAID® MSK

Hydrocolloid and SVP-based texturizer providing optimal viscoelastic balance and cooking stability for noodle strands.

  • Where Used: Pasta & Noodles
  • Activity: High Viscoelasticity Matrix
  • Nature: Hydrocolloid & SVP Based
  • Benefits: Enhances dough elasticity, cooking stability, and mouthfeel in noodles and pasta.
