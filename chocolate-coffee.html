Particulate Science & Emulsion Technology

Permanent Cocoa Suspension & Velvety Body for RTD Choco & Coffee

Insoluble cocoa particles and coffee solids possess high specific gravities that cause rapid settling, hard sediment compaction, and unsightly bottle ringing in RTD beverages during distribution.

Our STABAID CF® stabilizer provides an invisible, thixotropic hydrocolloid yield stress network that keeps dense cocoa solids in permanent suspension without creating heavy mouth feel or gelation. AIDFERM AF-F® eliminates stubborn foam during high-speed blending and canning.

Why RTD Beverage Brands Choose Praras

Advanced hydrocolloid networks engineered to suspend dense cocoa solids, prevent fat separation, and ensure smooth canning.

01

Zero Cocoa Sedimentation

Establishes a three-dimensional yield stress network that suspends dense insoluble cocoa powder particles across 12 months ambient storage.

02

Thermal Retort Stability

Maintains full suspension strength without gel breakdown or thinning during rigorous 121°C retort canning and UHT processing.

03

Velvety, Rich Texture

Imparts a luxurious, creamy mouthfeel and smooth chocolate coating that elevates consumer indulgence and mask bitterness.

04

Instant Process Defoaming

Suppresses heavy foam generated during high-shear cocoa powder dissolution, deaeration, and high-speed rotary canning lines.

05

Prevents Oil Neck Ringing

Protects dairy fats and added cocoa butter from coalescing, preventing unsightly fat rings on bottle necks and can lids.

06

FSSAI & Clean Conformance

Formulated exclusively from 100% food-grade, allergen-free botanical hydrocolloid gums conforming to FSSAI and international standards.
